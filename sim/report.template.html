Hayve

1,000 simulated people used Hayve. Here is what happened.

Each person has a country, age, income, interests, price sensitivity, tech comfort and patience. They signed up with their own address and used Shop, Bites, Ride, Stay, Drive and Hayve Pay through the same backend code the app runs. Every step was recorded with its result, the money involved and what the person thought. Money is simulated and shown in US dollars, the ledger's base currency.

Fixed before this report

Critical problems found and fixed

A 100-person pilot run found these. Each one is fixed, covered by a test and checked again in the 1,000-person run below.

Food could be ordered from a restaurant in another city

Found
In the pilot, 30 of 61 food orders went from people in London, Berlin, Dublin and Amsterdam to restaurants in New York. Bites showed every restaurant to everyone.
Fixed
Restaurants now have a city. Bites only lists restaurants in your delivery city and refuses orders from other cities. Where Bites isn't available yet, the screen says so and lists the cities it serves. Result: 0 cross-city orders in 1,000 people.

Bites showed a Dutch street address to every customer

Found
The Bites header said "Deliver to Cornelis Dirkszoonlaan 51" for everyone, including people in the US.
Fixed
The header now shows your own default delivery address, or your city if you haven't saved one.

A US restaurant promo was priced in euros

Found
"Free fries over €20" appeared on a New York restaurant for all customers, whatever their currency.
Fixed
The promo no longer contains a fixed currency amount. All prices on screen use the customer's currency.

Unclear message when a split was already fully paid

Found
Friends opening a split after everyone had paid saw "This request has already been paid", which reads like an error.
Fixed
It now says "Everyone has already paid their share. Nothing more to pay."
