What 1,000 people paid on top of the base price, why, and who got it

The base price is what the seller, restaurant, host or car owner asks, or the metered ride fare. Everything on top comes from a pricing rule and is shown as its own line before the customer pays. This page adds up every line from the 1,000-person simulation and explains why it applied. The simulated week ran Monday to Sunday with rush hours, weekend nights and five example events (a concert, a match, a festival, a hockey game and a marathon). Amounts in US dollars, simulated.
